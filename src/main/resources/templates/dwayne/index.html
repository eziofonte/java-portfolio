03 / How I approach thingsThe process

Think it through.
Then make it real.

  1. 01

    Understand.

    Start with the person using it. Ask what they need, where they get stuck, and what a simpler experience could look like.

  2. 02

    Explore.

    Sketch, arrange, and prototype. Work through the layout and interactions until the idea starts to make sense on screen.

  3. 03

    Build & refine.

    Bring it into the browser. Check the details, try it at different sizes, and keep improving how it feels to use.
